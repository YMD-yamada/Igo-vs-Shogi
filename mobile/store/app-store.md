• 囲碁 9×9 と将棋 5×5 が同時進行

## Description (EN)

Kuroshiro is an asymmetric board battle: Go and Shogi play by different rules on linked boards. Captures create interference on the opponent’s board.

• 9×9 Go and 5×5 Shogi  
• Hotseat (pass-and-play) and CPU  
• Tutorial and local handicaps
